# Privacy Policy — Window Ninja

Window Ninja does not collect, transmit, or share any data.

- All settings and preferences are stored locally in your browser and synced only through your own Chrome profile via `chrome.storage.sync`.
- The extension makes no network requests, includes no analytics, and contains no remote code.
- Erased-element selectors and per-site preferences are stored only in your browser's extension storage.

Contact: open an issue on the project repository.
